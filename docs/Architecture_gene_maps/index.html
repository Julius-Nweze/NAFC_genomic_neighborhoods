NAFC candidate-architecture gene-map atlases

Full atlas per architecture: every genome/locus qualifying for that signature, pooled across all three genome-source tiers (plant-root MAGs, OSPW-associated genomes, and NCBI reference genomes). Fig. 5, Fig. 6, Fig. S15, and Fig. S16 of the article show a representative subset of these maps. Most categories have two entries: a narrow "core" signature (a few specific genes) and a "full" signature using every reference-panel gene for that category; these are different gene sets. See Methods for thresholds and the literature-anchored versus data-driven distinction.
