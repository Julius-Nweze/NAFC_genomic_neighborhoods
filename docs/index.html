Methods

Proteins from 365 genomes were screened against a curated panel of 667 reference proteins in 22 functional categories with BLASTP (E-value ≤1×10−25, ≥30% identity, ≥50% query coverage). Best hits were linked to Prokka coordinates and grouped into candidate neighborhoods when located on the same contig with gaps of ≤12 genes and ≤25 kb between neighboring hits. Each neighborhood was expanded to include all intervening coding sequences. Arrows show gene orientation and colors show broad functional class.

The architecture atlas uses three kinds of signature:

  • Literature-anchored: gene combinations from characterized loci (bad_ali_chc, catechol_ortho, phenylacetate, protocatechuate, beta_ox_core).
  • Data-driven "core": the most frequently co-occurring genes within each category's multi-hit neighborhoods, typically ≥2 of the top four genes, adjusted per category. Hydroquinone is not included because its two genes co-occur in only one genome.
  • "Full" panel: every reference-panel gene of a category, with the smallest match threshold giving a legible number of genomes. Aromatics is also split into three subcategory groupings.

The threshold for each signature is given in its atlas title. Signature-qualified neighborhoods are candidate co-located gene sets. Gene order, strand, and orthology were not checked, so they are not confirmed operons, and genomic potential does not demonstrate activity.
